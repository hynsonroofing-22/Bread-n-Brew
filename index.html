Bago sa Pinoy? — new to Filipino baking?

Not sure what to order? Start here.

Pandesal

pan-de-SAL

The everyday Filipino bread roll. Soft, barely sweet, best warm. Split it with butter, or dunk it straight in your coffee — that is how most of us eat it at home.

Start with this one

Ensaymada

en-sigh-MA-da

A soft coiled brioche, brushed with butter and finished with sugar and grated cheese. Sweet and salty at once. Ours comes three ways: ube, yema or classic.

The one people come back for

Sapin-Sapin

SA-pin SA-pin

A layered rice cake made with coconut milk, each coloured layer its own flavour. Chewy, gently sweet, nothing like a Western cake. Weekends only.

Weekends, limited

Pancit Bihon

PAN-sit BEE-hon

Thin rice noodles stir-fried with vegetables and meat. A staple at every Filipino birthday, where the long noodles stand for a long life. Sold by the tub.

Feeds a few

Where it can be used: in store only, at 236 Onehunga Mall, Onehunga. It cannot be used for phone orders, delivery platforms, catering or bulk orders.

Limits: one code per customer. The code must be shown on your own device at the counter at the time of ordering — screenshots, copies, forwarded codes and codes shown after payment are not accepted. It cannot be combined with any other offer, promotion or discount, has no cash value, is not transferable and cannot be exchanged for cash or credit.

Validity: codes are dated and are intended for use on the day they are claimed.
